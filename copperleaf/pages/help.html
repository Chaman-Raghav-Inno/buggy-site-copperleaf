Fees and limits

How Copperleaf Bank handles your money. All amounts are in euros.

Accounts

  • Your total balance is everything you hold minus what you owe on your card.
  • Available money on the Everyday Account is the balance plus your arranged overdraft, minus card payments that are still pending.
  • The Rainy Day Saver has no overdraft: it can never go below €0. Three withdrawals a month are free; each one after that costs €2.

Transactions

  • Search ignores capital letters. Date filters include both the first and the last day you pick.
  • "Money in" and "Money out" add up every transaction that matches your filters, not just the page you're on.
  • Downloads show money out with a minus sign.

Payments

  • Amounts are in euros and cents (at most two decimal places). You can't pay an account into itself.
  • You can send up to €5,000 a day to payees in total. For the first 24 hours after you add a payee, you can send them up to €1,000.
  • Payments dated on a Saturday or Sunday leave your account on the next working day (Monday).
  • References can be up to 18 characters.
  • Monthly payments set for the 29th, 30th or 31st go out on the last day of shorter months.
  • "Due in the next 30 days" adds up your active scheduled payments only.

Payees

  • We check every IBAN, including its check digits, before you can pay it.
  • Each IBAN can only be saved once.
  • Deleting a payee also cancels any scheduled payments to them.

Copper Card

  • Credit limit €2,000. Your monthly spending limit can be set from €50 to €2,000.
  • The minimum payment is 3% of your statement balance, or €25 if that's more.
  • Payments in other currencies are converted at the rate shown and carry a 2.75% non-euro fee, shown as its own line.
  • A frozen card stays frozen until you unfreeze it.

Statements

  • Each statement covers one calendar month, from the 1st to the last day. Closing balance = opening + money in − money out, and the next month opens where the last one closed.
  • Interest is paid on the last day of the month and counts as money in.
  • The statement download has only that account's transactions.

Disputes

  • You can dispute a card payment (money out) from the last 120 days, once.
  • When a dispute is upheld, the money goes back to the card that was charged.
  • You'll see our reply to your dispute. Notes our staff keep for themselves stay private.

Security and settings

  • Passwords need at least 10 characters including a number, and must differ from your current one.
  • After 5 wrong passwords in a row, logging in is locked for 15 minutes.
  • Low-balance alerts can be set to any amount in euros and cents, and go to a valid email address of your choice.
  • Arranged overdrafts go up to €2,000.
